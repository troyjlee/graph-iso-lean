# Lean development plan, milestone 2: a polynomial-time group backend

Prepared 19 October 2026. **Status: complete** (`lean/docs/status.md`, theorem map in
`lean/docs/theorem-map.md`). The checks delivered are narrower than the verification list
below; `lean/docs/status.md` (milestone 2, item 5) records the difference. This continues `PLAN.md`, whose milestone (string isomorphism with
costed group operations) is complete at commit c084d83. The conventions, cost model, contracts
and standards of `PLAN.md` and `lean/docs/` remain in force.

## Objective and completion criteria

Replace the enumeration backend by a verified backend whose charges are polynomial in the
degree and the number of generators. Milestone 1 already proves the solver and the reductions
against any backend meeting the contracts, so they then hold with a per-invocation unit `Q`
polynomial in `n`.

The milestone is complete when:

1. **Implementation.** A backend `ssImpl` over the empty signature implements all eight group
   operations, including `mem`. It is well-formed (`WfImpl`) and computes an oracle
   `ssOracle` that meets every contract (`MeetsContract`).
2. **Charges.** A schedule `ssB` bounds each implementation's cost (`Implements`). It is
   accompanied by explicit polynomial bounds:
   - `generate` from `L` generators: polynomial in `n` and `L`;
   - `stabilizer`, `kernel`, `lift`, `mem`, `orbits` and `orbitTransversal` on a group with
     `s` generators: polynomial in `n` and `s`;
   - `cosetReps`: polynomial in `n`, the two generator counts and the index `[G : K]`. Its
     output has `[G : K]` elements.
3. **Bounds.** `ssBounds : BackendBounds ssCtx ssDV n (n * n) G₀` holds for every ambient
   group `G₀`, with charge bounds independent of `|G₀|`.
4. **Concrete theorems.** These are analogous to milestone 1's, with no assumed oracle:
   - `solve_ss` and `solve_ss_checked`: correctness, and cost at most `P(n, L) +
     solveBound Q m N` with `Q` an explicit polynomial in `n`;
   - `restrict_ss` and `blockReduce_ss`.
5. **Checks.**
   - Kernel-evaluated runs.
   - An interpreter comparison with brute force and with the reference backend.
   - Interpreter runs on groups far beyond enumeration, such as `S₁₀`, wreath products and
     the Mathieu group `M₁₁`, with group orders confirmed from the tables.
6. **Audit and docs.** The axiom audit is extended; the theorem map, backend contracts and
   status are updated, together with the list of remaining work.

This milestone makes no claim about the solver's own recursion. Its bound `solveBound Q m N`
stays linear in the group order `N`. Luks's bounded-composition-factor strategy and Babai's
recursion belong to later milestones (`lean/docs/efficient-backends.md`).

## Design

### Backend data

The backend data is `D = Ty.unit`, with the trivial invariant `ssDV n gs () := True`.

- Every operation that needs a stabilizer chain builds one from the group's generators.
- Stored chains would need a data invariant carried through every reduction and every output
  group. Rebuilding costs only a polynomial factor.
- Caching a chain is an optional later refinement. It would change `D` and `ssDV`, not the
  contracts.

### Sims tables with full bases

**Base order.** A base order `b` is a duplicate-free array of all `n` points: a permutation of
`0, …, n − 1`. Since the base is full, the residue of a successful sift fixes every point,
so it is the identity, and no final identity test is needed.

**Table.** A table is an `n × n` array of `Option PermData`.

- Slot `(k, j)` holds an element that fixes `b₀, …, b_{k−1}` and maps `b_k` to `j`.
- Slot `(k, b_k)` holds the identity.
- `T_k` denotes the elements in row `k`.

**Products.** `Γ_k = T_k T_{k+1} ⋯ T_{n−1}` is the set of products with one factor per row,
and `Γ_n = {1}`.

**Sifting** from row `k` (for `x` fixing `b₀, …, b_{k−1}`):

1. Look up slot `(k, x b_k)`.
2. If it is empty, fail with residue `x` at row `k`.
3. Otherwise continue from row `k + 1` with `σ⁻¹ x`.

`x ∈ Γ_k` exactly when the sift succeeds. This uses only the slot structure; `Γ_k` need not
be a group.

**Closure criterion** (Sims; Knuth, *Efficient representation of perm groups*, 1991).
Suppose `τ σ ∈ Γ_k` whenever `σ ∈ T_k`, `τ ∈ T_j` and `j ≥ k`. Then each `Γ_k` is closed
under multiplication, so in a finite group it is the subgroup generated by `T_k, …, T_{n−1}`.
The proof is by downward induction on `k`:

- `τ σ γ = σ' (γ' γ)`;
- then use `Subgroup.closure_toSubmonoid_of_finite`.

**Completeness.** Suppose further that every entry lies in `G` and every generator of `G`
lies in `Γ₀`. Then:

- `Γ₀ = G`;
- `Γ_k = G^{(k)}`, the pointwise stabilizer of `b₀, …, b_{k−1}` in `G`;
- slot `(k, j)` is filled exactly when `j` is in the `G^{(k)}`-orbit of `b_k`.

### Construction: a worklist with a slot-filling potential

The state has three parts:

- the table;
- the list of entries `(k, σ)`;
- a pending list of items `(k, x)`, each an element to sift from row `k`.

Initially the table holds the identities, and the pending list holds `(0, g)` for every
generator `g`.

Processing an item sifts it. When the sift fails at row `k'` with residue `h`:

1. Insert `h` into its empty slot `(k', h b_{k'})`.
2. Queue `(l, h σ)` for every entry `σ` at row `l ≤ k'`.
3. Queue `(k', τ h)` for every entry `τ` at row `l ≥ k'`.

**Invariants.**

- Entries lie in `G` and respect the slot conditions.
- The entry list lists the filled slots.
- Every pair of entries whose product the criterion needs, and every generator, is either in
  `Γ` or pending.

Successful sifts stay successful as the table grows, since filled slots never change. At the
end the pending list is empty, so the criterion holds.

**Termination and cost.** No group order is needed.

- Each insertion fills one of at most `n²` slots and queues at most `2n²` items.
- `Φ = |pending| + 2n² (n² − E)`, with `E` the number of filled slots, drops at every
  iteration. So there are at most `L + 2n⁴` iterations.
- A sift costs `O(n²)` and an insertion `O(n³)`, for a total of `O(L n² + n⁶)` in the cost
  model.

**Outputs.** Output groups are generated by table entries: at most `n²` per table, which
gives `s = n²`. No separate filter (Sims's, Jerrum's) is needed.

### The operations

| Operation | Algorithm | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `generate` | table for base `0, …, n − 1` | all entries |
| `stabilizer u` | table for a base starting with `u` | entries in rows `≥ 1` |
| `kernel W` | table for a base listing `W` first | entries in rows `≥ |W|` |
| `lift W π'` | same table; in row `i < |W|` choose the entry with the required image | the product |
| `mem π` | table for base `0, …, n − 1`; sift `π` | success |
| `orbits W` | search from each unvisited window point, applying generators, with a visited mask | the orbits |
| `orbitTransversal u` | search from `u`, with `r_{g v} = g r_v` | pairs `(v, r_v)` |
| `cosetReps G K` | search over left cosets from `K`: `g r` is new unless `r'⁻¹ g r ∈ K` for a found `r'`, tested by sifting in `K`'s table | the representatives |

The orbit searches use only the generators. Forward closure under the generators is the
orbit, by `isInvariant_closure_iff`; the coset search uses the same finiteness argument on
cosets.

## Implementation phases and acceptance gates

| Phase | Work | Acceptance gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Plan; executable functional prototype of sifting and the construction | `#eval` on `S₈`, `A₈`, `M₁₁`, a wreath product: orders and timings as expected |
| 1 | Sims-table theory: tables, `Γ_k`, sifting, the closure criterion, completeness consequences | Theorems for arbitrary tables satisfying the criterion; no algorithm needed |
| 2 | Functional construction: invariants, potential, final completeness, entry count | `schreierSims n b gs` is a complete table for `⟨gs⟩` with at most `n²` entries, within the iteration bound |
| 3 | Programs for sifting and the construction, with costs and well-formedness | `RunsWithin` to the functional models within explicit polynomial bounds |
| 4 | The eight operations: models, contracts, programs, costs | `ssOracle` meets every contract; each program computes it within its bound |
| 5 | Backend wiring and instantiations | `ssImpl_implements`, `ssBounds`, `solve_ss`, `solve_ss_checked`, `restrict_ss`, `blockReduce_ss` |
| 6 | Checks, audit, documentation | Kernel runs, sweep, large-group runs, axiom listing, docs |

Re-estimate after phase 2, when the proof cost of the invariants is visible.

## Proposed layout

New modules under `lean/GraphIso/Sims/`:

- `Table.lean` — tables, `Γ_k`, sifting, the closure criterion (phase 1).
- `Construct.lean` — the worklist model, invariants and termination (phase 2).
- `SiftProg.lean`, `ConstructProg.lean` — programs and costs (phase 3).
- `Orbits.lean`, `Ops.lean`, `OpsProg.lean` — the operations (phase 4).

Backend wiring goes in `lean/GraphIso/Solver/Sims.lean` and `SimsCompose.lean` (phase 5),
and checks in `lean/GraphIsoChecks/Sims.lean` (phases 0 and 6).

## Verification

**Kernel-evaluated runs** on small groups, tied to the concrete theorems:

- the trivial group, `S₃`, `C₄`, `D₄`, `A₄`, `S₄`;
- an intransitive group;
- a degree-5 group.

**Interpreter sweep** at degrees up to 5:

- `solve_ss` answers compared with brute force and with the reference backend, as denoted
  sets;
- costs checked against the proved bounds.

**Large groups**, in the interpreter:

- `S_n` and `A_n` up to `n = 10`, `M₁₁` on 11 points, `S₄ ≀ S₃` on 12 points;
- group orders computed from table row sizes must equal the known orders;
- solver runs on these groups with string pairs whose answers are known (a string with
  distinct symbols, or a constant string).

**Axiom audit** as in milestone 1, with the new principal theorems listed by name.
